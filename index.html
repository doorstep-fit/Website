Common questions

Do I need gym equipment at home?

Not necessarily! Our trainers can help you get started with bodyweight exercises and available equipment. We’ll recommend suitable workout options based on your space and fitness goals.

Can I start as a complete beginner?

Absolutely! Our personal training is suitable for all fitness levels, including complete beginners. Your trainer will guide you step by step with a personalised workout plan.

Which areas of Hyderabad do you cover?

We provide at-home personal training services across selected areas of Hyderabad. Share your neighbourhood or PIN code through WhatsApp to check trainer availability.

Can two people train together?

Yes! You can choose our Couple Training package and enjoy personalised workout sessions together with your partner.

How long is each session?

Each personal training session lasts 60 minutes, giving you dedicated time for warm-up, workout and cool-down.

What if I need to reschedule?

No problem! You can request a session reschedule by informing us in advance. Rescheduling is subject to trainer availability and our booking policy.

What services does DoorStep.fit offer?

We offer 1-on-1 Home Personal Training, Online Fitness Coaching, Couple Training and Nutrition Coaching to help you achieve your fitness goals.

How can I book a free trial?

Simply click on the Book Your Free Trial button, submit your details and our team will contact you to discuss your fitness goals and schedule.
