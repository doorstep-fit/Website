Privacy

This notice describes the consultation tool on the Doorstep site preview.

Information you enter

The tool uses your name, selected training option, goal, and preferred time to create a text file in your browser.

How the brief is handled

The tool saves the brief to your device. It does not send the form entries to a booking service.

Managing your brief

You can edit or delete the downloaded file. Anyone you share it with can read the details it contains.
