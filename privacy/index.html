Privacy Policy

At TheDoorStep.fit, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us or submit an enquiry, we may collect:

  • Name and mobile number
  • Location or area
  • Fitness goals and training preferences
  • Preferred training schedule
  • Relevant health, injury or fitness information voluntarily shared with us

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and consultation requests.
  • Understand your fitness goals and requirements.
  • Recommend suitable training programmes.
  • Schedule and manage training sessions.
  • Communicate service updates and payment information.

3. Information Sharing

Your personal information will not be sold or rented to third parties. We may share relevant information with assigned trainers and service providers when necessary to deliver our services.

4. Data Protection

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, misuse or disclosure.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary for service delivery, business records and applicable legal requirements.

6. Your Privacy Rights

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, or withdraw consent for its use, subject to applicable legal requirements.
